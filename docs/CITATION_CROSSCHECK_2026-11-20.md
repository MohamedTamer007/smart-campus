# Smart Campus — mechanical citation crosscheck (updated 20 November 2026)

**Source of truth:** [native Smart Campus thesis](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1UWZoMqGwvjm_V_H6efziZfP8bCW1nZLODHFAOrRQu1Q/edit), chapters 1–6 and the 91 registered bibliography entries.

**Updated screening result:** 47/91 author–year proximity candidates, 44/91 still flagged for individual review. This count increased from an earlier 26/91 after editorial corrections of confirmed author/title/year forms. It is not a scholarly citation certification, an APA 7 pass rate, or a count of valid publications.

**Method:** Accents are normalized, and an exact first-author surname and publication year are searched within roughly one line-length of narrative text. References may be genuinely cited by short title, coauthor, collective author, grouped parenthetical citation, or cross-reference even when this rule returns no match. Conversely a false positive may result from accidental proximity. Each claim and the linked publisher metadata must be checked manually. No citation was invented merely to change this metric.
