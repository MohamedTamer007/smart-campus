**Method and limitation:** Match each recorded first-author surname and parenthesized publication year within approximately one sentence, with accent normalization. This is a **screening heuristic, not APA 7 verification or proof of citation validity**. Title-based citations, grouped citations, author-name variants and attribution mistakes can cause false negatives or positives. A missing match does not establish an uncited source; a matched author-year does not establish correct publisher data.
